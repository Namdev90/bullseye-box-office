# Oktoberfest Goa source check

Checked on 5 November 2026. The app's **Oktoberfest 2027 is fictional**. No official 2027 dates, lineup, venue plan or announcement were established by this research.

## Verified and viewed

- [District's Oktoberfest Goa 2026 ticket page](https://www.district.in/events/oktoberfest-goa-2026-oct30-2026-buy-tickets) was accessible. It identifies the 2026 event, dates of 30 October–1 November, Panaji venue and ended status. Its page data identifies the publisher/brand as **BULLSEYE ENTERTAINMENT**. This is a primary ticketing publication, rather than an aggregator's re-created artwork.
- The event page directly links the unchanged [2026 cover artwork](https://cdn.district.in/assets/events/publisher/event_cover_image_horizontal/01M1ZVGSB9JND7YHBX9NNE0ZYT.jpg), downloaded for local inspection outside the public artifact. Visually inspected: it is a lineup composition with Bullseye branding, “GOA 2026”, artist names and photographs, not an unbranded event photograph. **Do not relabel, crop into, or present it as a 2027 announcement or lineup.**
- Its [gallery asset](https://cdn.district.in/assets/events/publisher/event_gallery/01M4T2YFP690WHQ1QH2Q0JQG86.jpg), downloaded for local inspection outside the public artifact, was also downloaded unchanged and visually inspected. It is a venue-layout illustration, not a verified 2027 plan or documentary aerial photograph.
- [BotLab Dynamics' own account of its 2026 drone show](https://www.botlabdynamics.com/blogs/oktoberfest-goa-2026-drone-show) independently identifies Bull's Eye Entertainment and the 2026 edition. Its photographs remain BotLab/third-party material; none were downloaded or added to the app.
- [GEVA's member listing](https://www.geva.online/) displays the [genuine Bullseye logo already in this project](https://www.geva.online/images/member-logo/50-bullseye-entertainment.jpg?v=12). This is an association reference, with current-master status still unconfirmed.

These two District files are **2026 archival references only**, retained outside the published source and deployment artifact. No app screen references them. The user authorized Bullseye logo/photo use for the public prototype; that does not establish a general redistribution licence or ownership of every third-party portrait or map element. Public accessibility does not make an asset copyright-free. No artwork was altered and no SharePass logo was invented or published.
